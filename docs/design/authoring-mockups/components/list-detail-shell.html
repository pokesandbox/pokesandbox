Rules

  • The URL names the selected item (/regions/:id/build/locations/route-1). On phones that URL shows the detail screen; on md+ it shows both panes.
  • Phone app bar: back (44px), title plus one context line, one or two icon actions. The back arrow returns to the list with its scroll and filters kept.
  • Children sit under their parent, indented 24px. A Region Health dot sits before the chevron.
  • Edits autosave per field; the app bar shows Saving… then Saved.
  • md (768): list 360px plus detail. lg (1024): the builder sidebar appears and persists across every builder screen. Below lg the sidebar is the builder home list.
  • Empty list: one sentence and the primary action ("No locations yet. Add your first town or route." + Add location).
