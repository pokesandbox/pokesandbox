Notes

  • One view, many doors: the info button on species picker rows (opens it without selecting), the image in a Pokédex slot, each form row in a slot, encounter slots and party Pokémon.
  • Rules-aware: stats, types, moves and fields follow the region's rules preset. Classic Gen 1 shows one Special stat and hides abilities, gender and held items; a form whose type the rules don't have says so.
  • Sections: header (image placeholder, name, National number, region slot, types) · base stats as bars · details · evolution line (with a shortcut to add a missing evolution) · forms side by side with what differs · level-up moves · TMs/HMs from this region's case · where it's used in this region.
  • Read-only. Edits happen where you came from; Close returns there with nothing lost. "Select Meowth" appears only when opened from a picker.
  • Phones: a full-screen sheet with the name pinned at the top. Desktop: a 480px side panel, so the list or slot stays visible.
  • Needs the catalog rework (#120, #157): today's DexEntry has no Zod schema, and rules-aware stats and learnsets come from there.
