PokeSandbox authoring mockups

Authoring UI mockups

Mid-fidelity mockups for the PokeSandbox webapp, exported from the design system. Phone frames are drawn at 390×844 and desktop frames at 1440; ★ screens include a dark version. Use the toggle in the top bar to switch the whole page to dark mode.

PokeSandbox is a non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures or The Pokémon Company.
