  • Each connection shows its label (North, Through the gate), the destination with its kind tile, and a "Requires…" badge if it's gated. Rows reorder like any SortableList; the order is the order of travel buttons in Discord.
  • "Two-way" adds the matching connection on the destination; the line under the switch names it. Turning it off later doesn't delete the other side.
  • "Requirement" is the condition summary row (K1): empty for a new connection, and Add opens the condition editor (K2). Once a requirement is set, the gate options appear under it, as in K3.
  • Delete lives in the location's More menu. The confirm counts inbound connections ("3 connections lead here and will be removed") and names them.
