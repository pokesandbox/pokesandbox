Notes

  • Method tabs come from the region's method registry; each shows its encounter rate. "+ Method" adds one this location supports (Surf only appears in the list when the region has it).
  • The rate is the chance that one Explore finds a Pokémon. It's per method, per location.
  • Each slot: species (SearchCombobox), min and max level, weight. The % is weight ÷ total weight, rounded to whole numbers, and updates as you type. Both views here are live.
  • Validation sits on the row: a red tint plus the sentence under the fields ("Min level is above max."). Errors also appear in Region Health and block publish.
  • Reorder with the handle or More → Move up/down. Order only affects display; weights decide odds.
  • The totals footer stays pinned: slot count, total weight, and a warning when the table has no slots or a zero total.
  • Conditional tables (night, swarms) need the condition builder, so "Add conditional table" is shown as coming soon.
