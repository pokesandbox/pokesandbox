  • Where it lives: one Explore tab, in this order: the method tabs and the selected method's rate, requirement and tables; then "Also found by exploring here" (counts that jump to their section); then Hidden items; then Traversal cost (R2). The "Hidden items: 3" count scrolls to the section.
  • What the docs define: a hidden item is an Explore outcome tracked by pickup:<key>:taken (doc 04, doc 10). It joins Explore's outcomes on methods that allow trainers and items (grass, cave), never on Surf or the rods.
  • Design proposal, not in the docs yet: the item (TMs included), a quantity, an optional condition (K1), and Can be found: Once per journey (default), or Again after a while for berries and gems, every N hours or days. The cadence is the server's call; there's no built-in minimum. Renewable pickups would store when they were taken, not just a flag.
  • Remove confirms first; players who already found it keep it.
  • Desktop: the same sections in the detail pane; the sheet becomes the side panel (K2).
