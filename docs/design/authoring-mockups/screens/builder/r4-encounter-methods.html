  • Where: Settings → Encounter methods. A location’s Explore tab links to the method its outcomes come from.
  • The list starts with Walk in the grass, Explore the cave, Surf, Old Rod, Good Rod, Super Rod and Look around. Each row shows an icon placeholder, the method’s outcomes, and its requirement.
  • Outcomes: each method has its own table of what an explore can find: wild Pokémon, a trainer, or a hidden item, each with a percentage. "+ Outcome" adds a kind the table doesn’t have; any row can be removed, but a method needs at least one.
  • The total must be 100%. Edits never change other rows. Over or under 100%, the total turns red, the error says how much to remove or add, and the method can’t be saved.
  • Running out: when a location has no trainers left to beat or items left to find, their share is spread over the remaining rows. A method with nothing left to find hides its button in Discord; a renewing item coming back brings the button back.
  • Look around is how towns and buildings offer hidden items and trainers: no requirement, Trainer 50 · Item 50 with no Wild row, and not affected by Repel.
  • Locations use these tables unless they customize their own, which is where events can be added.
  • Custom methods ("Search the tall reeds") work the same as the seeded ones. Deleting a method lists the locations that use it; their tables for it are removed.
