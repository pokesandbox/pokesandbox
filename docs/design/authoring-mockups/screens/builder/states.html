Notes

  • Conflict: each edit is a command with the entity's version. If someone else saved first, the command is refused and the banner appears under the app bar, naming who. Nothing is overwritten. Reload fetches their version; the field you were typing in keeps your text until you leave it, and "Copy my text" puts it on the clipboard.
  • Inline error: the field gets a red border and a sentence under it. The field doesn't save while it's invalid; the app bar says "1 field to fix" instead of "Saved".
  • Save indicator: Saving… appears only after 300ms so quick saves don't flicker, then Saved. A failed save keeps the edit locally and offers Retry.
  • Toast: bottom of the screen, above the safe area. Success clears after 4s; errors stay until Retry or dismiss.
  • Screen readers: the save indicator is a polite live region; the conflict banner is an alert.
