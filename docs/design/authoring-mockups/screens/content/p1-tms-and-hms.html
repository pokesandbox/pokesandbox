Notes

  • Numbers come from order. TMs are numbered TM01, TM02… down the list and HMs HM01… in their own section. Reordering (handle, or More → Move up/down) renumbers, and the header warns if a released number would change.
  • Each row: the number, the move (move SearchCombobox), and an HM switch. Turning HM on moves the row to the HM section.
  • Single use or reusable follows the rules preset: in Classic Gen 1 a TM is used up and an HM is reusable, so the badge is read-only here. Presets with reusable TMs change the badge.
  • Fill from Gen 1 defaults adds TM01–TM50 and HM01–HM05 with their usual moves. In a case that already has rows it's in the More menu and asks before replacing.
  • Items given in templates (N2) find these by number or move: "TM34 Bide".
