  • Numbers are stored, gaps are fine, nothing renumbers (doc 12). The list is always sorted by number, TMs then HMs. There's no reordering, so a player's TM24 always means the same move.
  • Removing a machine leaves an empty slot ("TM25 · empty", with Assign a move). It confirms first: players who have it won't see it until a move is assigned again. Region Health lists empty slots as info, not warnings, and the publish review lists slots this release empties.
  • Add picks the next free number for TM or HM; the number can be changed to any free number, and a taken number shows an error next to the field. Each row's More menu has Change number and Remove.
  • Single use or reusable is a region rule (tmReusable, doc 18): TMs follow it, HMs are always reusable. The badge is read-only and says "Set by your region's rules", linking to Settings → Rules.
  • Fill from Gen 1 defaults adds TM01–TM50 and HM01–HM05 with their usual moves. In a case that already has machines it's in the More menu and only fills empty numbers.
