  • Matches today's Discord builder: numbered slots, up to the number of species your rules allow, each holding one species. A slot can be added, swapped, removed, or customised (whether the base form is included, plus which other forms and in what order). Empty slots are allowed and show as gaps you can fill.
  • Search finds by name or number. Rows show the number, a neutral image placeholder, the species, and its forms ("+1 form").
  • Reorder with the handle or More → Move up/down. Moving renumbers the slots in between, and the sheet says which numbers change before you confirm.
  • Add from National Dex range fills many slots at once, for example #001–#151, skipping slots that already have a species unless you say otherwise. The summary says exactly what will happen.
  • Species info: "View species info" under the species, and an info button on every form row, open the shared Species info view (stats, evolutions, forms, moves, where it's used) without leaving the slot. Alolan Meowth's row would also show a warning here, because its Dark type isn't in Classic Gen 1 rules.
