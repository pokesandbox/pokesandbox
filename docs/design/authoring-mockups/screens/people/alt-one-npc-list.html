  • An alternative to L1 + M1. The sidebar has one "NPCs" entry; Trainers goes away. Filters: All · Trainers · Non-battle · Battle only. Picking Trainers adds the class chips from L1.
  • One editor, four tabs: Person (who, where, talk) · Battle (class, AI, payout, format, engagement, after defeat) · Party · Lines. Party and Lines are the L2 tabs unchanged. An NPC who doesn't battle shows Person and Battle only; Battle explains and offers "Make battleable", which creates the trainer and reveals Party and Lines.
  • No linking step. The data stays as doc 11 decided (an NPC references a Trainer), but the builder creates and names the trainer for you. The two edge cases become explicit, rarer actions:
  • Shared trainer (one Rocket Grunt team behind several NPCs): Battle → More → "Use an existing trainer". A banner then says "Shared with 3 other NPCs. Changes apply to all of them." with "Make a copy for this NPC".
  • Battle only (a trainer used only by a script, like a forced ambush): listed with a "Battle only" badge. Its Person tab says "Not placed anywhere. Used by script mt-moon-ambush." with "Give them a presence".
  • Sprite lives on Person; Battle uses it unless the trainer class overrides it.
