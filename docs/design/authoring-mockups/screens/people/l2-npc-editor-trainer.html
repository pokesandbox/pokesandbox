Notes

  • One editor for an NPC who battles: Person (name, sprite, where they are, talk) · Battle (class, how the battle starts, AI, payout, format, after defeat) · Party · Lines. An NPC who doesn't battle has only Person and Battle, and Battle offers "Make battleable".
  • Name placeholders live on Person: the hint chip inserts {name:rival}, the name the player gave their rival. The trainer uses the NPC's name and sprite.
  • Party variants are ordered cards, each with a condition (K1). First match wins. Collapse the ones you're not editing. Add, Duplicate, reorder by handle or More → Move up/down.
  • The bag is per variant, following doc 11 (parties[].items), so a later team can carry better items.
  • Warning: no variant is unconditioned, so if none match the battle can't start. Region Health shows the same.

Notes

  • Payout: Formula is the default and shows its result for the first variant; Fixed swaps in a number field.
  • Moves: empty slots show what the engine will pick ("Auto: Growl", from the last four level-up moves). Override sets a move; Reset returns to auto.
  • Held item is shown but off: Classic Gen 1 rules have no held items. Rules presets with them turn it on.
  • Lines: intro and defeat are passages with a Discord preview that fills placeholders with a sample name. What they say if you talk to them afterwards is the after-defeat script on Battle.
