Notes

  • Open and Dismissed are tabs under the header. Open is the default and is what every count uses: the badges here, the builder home card, the sidebar badge, and the dots on rows.
  • Groupings: By severity (Errors, then Warnings) or By area (Locations, Encounters, NPCs). Errors block publish; warnings don't.
  • Each item: what's wrong, why it matters and what to do, the path to the exact field, and Fix, which opens that field (the same deep link as Discord's "Open in builder"; the validator's subject is precise enough, doc 16).
  • Info items (an empty TM slot) come after warnings, aren't counted as warnings, and can't be dismissed. They never block publishing.
  • Only warnings can be dismissed. Dismiss asks for an optional reason, then shows an Undo toast. It hides that one warning, not the whole check.
  • A dismissal comes back on its own when the facts behind it change (Route 22 gains or loses a connection), and the item returns to Open as new.
  • Dismissed lists who dismissed each warning, when and why, with Restore. Dismissals are shared by everyone editing the region.
  • Checks run on every save and on publish; Re-run checks is there after big imports.
  • All clear means no open items; it still mentions dismissed warnings so they're never invisible.
