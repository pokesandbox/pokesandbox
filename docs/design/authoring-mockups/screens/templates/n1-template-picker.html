Notes

  • "Add" on the NPCs list asks one question: start blank, or from a template. Templates are forms that create the NPC, its trainer, its script and any rewards in one go, so most authors never see a raw step list.
  • Each card says what it's for and what it creates. Starter Gift, Gym Leader, Gift NPC and Forced Encounter are designed; Route Trainer opens a "not designed yet" note in this mockup. Starter Gift is the one being opened here.
  • Gauntlet and In-game Trade come with the full step editor, and regions with trials instead of gyms will want a Trial Captain template; they're shown as "Also planned" so the list doesn't look final.
  • There's no talker template: someone who just talks is a Talk script of Text steps.
  • Templates can also start from other places later (a location's More menu, Region Health fixes), opening with the location filled in.
