Notes

  • "Add" on the NPCs list asks one question: start blank, or from a template. Templates are forms that create the NPC, its trainer, its script and any rewards in one go, so most authors never see a raw step list.
  • Each card says what it's for and what it creates. Gym Leader is the only one designed this round; the others are available but open a "not designed yet" note in this mockup.
  • Doc 07 also lists Gauntlet, In-game Trade and Simple Talker; they're shown as "Also planned" so the list doesn't look final.
  • Templates can also start from other places later (a location's More menu, Region Health fixes), opening with the location filled in.
