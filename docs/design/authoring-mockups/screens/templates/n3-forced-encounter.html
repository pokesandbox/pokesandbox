Notes

  • Same pattern as Gym Leader: short steps on phones with Continue pinned, a review of what it creates, then the created item with "Made with the Forced Encounter template" and Edit as form.
  • Where: the location, plus optionally only when arriving from chosen places.
  • Who: an existing trainer, or Create a trainer, which opens the party editor. The rival shows its three starter-based teams.
  • When: Only once is on by default; an optional condition, like only after Oak's Parcel.
  • Battle: "Players can lose without blacking out" (on for the rival) and whether to heal the party afterwards.
  • Text: before the battle, after a win, after a loss, with the Discord preview filling in sample names.
  • It creates an Enters event on the location and its script, and uses the chosen trainer; it doesn't copy the trainer.
