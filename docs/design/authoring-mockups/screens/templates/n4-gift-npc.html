Notes

  • Who: an existing NPC, or a new one with a name and sprite, placed at a location.
  • Gift: one kind per gift. Items with quantities (TMs included, found by number or move), or a Pokémon: species, level, an optional nickname, and a held item when the rules allow held items (Classic Gen 1 doesn't).
  • When: an optional condition. "Given once per journey" is fixed.
  • Text: offering the gift, what they say the next time you talk, and what they say when the bag or party is full. What happens when the party is full follows the region's rules, shown read-only.
  • Review says what it creates, then the NPC opens with "Made with the Gift NPC template" and Edit as form.
