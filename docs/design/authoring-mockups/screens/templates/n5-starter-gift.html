Notes

  • Same pattern as the other templates: short steps on phones with Continue pinned, one long form on desktop, a review of what it creates, then the created item with "Made with the Starter Gift template".
  • Who and where: an existing NPC (Oak) and the location they hand out the choice (Oak's Lab), or a new NPC.
  • Options: 2 to 6 Pokémon, one row each with species and level. Remove a row, or "+ Option" up to six.
  • The rival's pick: Off, or "The rival takes the one strong against yours". When it's on, the mapping fills in from types and stays editable, row by row.
  • Remembers the choice in a choice progression, new ("Starter") or existing. Its values are the options. With the rival's pick on, the rival's pick is remembered too, in "Rival's starter" by default, and the rival's party variants key off that.
  • Text: before the choice, one confirmation line per option ("So, you want {species:charmander}?", shown as "Charmander"), and after the choice. The name comes from the species, so it's right in every language.
  • It creates one script and one Talk script on Oak, and sets the Starter and Rival's starter progressions. The Talk script runs only until a starter is chosen, so Oak's other Talk scripts take over afterwards.
